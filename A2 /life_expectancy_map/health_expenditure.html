Global Burden of Communicable Diseases

This dashboard explores the global impact of communicable diseases through Disability-Adjusted Life Years (DALYs), measuring years lost to premature mortality and years lived with disability across different countries and time periods.

Understanding Disease Burden Patterns

The map visualization shows how communicable disease burden varies globally. Countries with darker red colors and larger circles indicate higher DALY values, representing greater health impact from diseases such as respiratory infections, HIV/AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria. Use the year slider to observe how these patterns have evolved over time.
